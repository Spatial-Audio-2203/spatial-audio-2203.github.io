Tianshu Qu

Associate Professor

Tianshu Qu is an Associate Professor at the School of Intelligence Science and Technology, Peking University, with an additional affiliation at PKU's National Biomedical Imaging Center. His research covers spatial hearing, virtual sound, 3D audio scene analysis and multichannel audio coding.

He received his PhD in Communication and Information Systems from Jilin University in 2002 and completed postdoctoral research at PKU. He joined the PKU faculty in 2004 and visited Michigan State University in 2011–2012.

He teaches Signals and Systems, Machine Perception Laboratory, and the graduate course Spatial Hearing and Audio/Music Processing.

Overview

TWNM organizes auditory scene analysis into perception, relational integration and reasoning. It combines spatial representations from first-order Ambisonics with semantic audio features and trains progressively to answer spatial scene questions. Evaluation uses a controlled benchmark generated from scene metadata.

Overview

Flow-HOA targets higher-order Ambisonics encoding from sparse, irregular arrays. It generates FIR encoding filters offline with joint time, frequency and spatial objectives. The resulting filters can be applied without running the generator. The paper reports simulated-data evaluations and listening tests on real recordings.
